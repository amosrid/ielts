ROADMAP PHASES

14 STAGES
FASE 1 • BASE CHARACTER CREATION Reward: +300 XP

Fondasi Kerangka Kalimat

Hentikan glitch grammar dasar! Bangun kerangka Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) yang kokoh sebelum menyentuh tata bahasa rumit.

STAGE 1

Kerangka Kalimat Inggris (SVO)

Urutan kata baku: Subject + Verb + Object/Complement. Mencegah kebiasaan menerjemahkan kata demi kata secara harfiah.

[Subject] + [Verb] + [Object]
"Students (S) achieve (V) high scores (O)."
STAGE 2

Lexical vs Auxiliary & "Be" Anchor

Membedakan kata kerja aksi dan "Be" penanda kondisi. Menghilangkan bug seperti *"They confused"*.

They confused about rules.
They are confused about rules.
STAGE 3

Auxiliary "Do" - Negatives & Questions

Penggunaan Do / Does / Did untuk kalimat negatif & tanya tanpa mencampurkan Do dengan Be.

Positive: "They fulfill obligations."
Negative: "They do not fulfill obligations."
FASE 2 • TIME SYSTEMS & MODIFIERS Reward: +400 XP

Sistem Waktu & Modifikasi Kalimat

Menguasai tenses dasar, modal auxiliary, serta penambahan presisi melalui artikel fonetik dan preposisi akurat.

STAGE 4

Present & Past Simple Fluency

Penguasaan verba irregular dan perubahan bentuk waktu untuk Speaking Part 1-2 & Task 1 Overview.

Present: "They manage resources."
Past: "They managed resources yesterday."
STAGE 5

Continuous Tenses & Future Forms

Penggunaan Continuous & Future (will/predicted to) untuk tren data grafik IELTS Task 1.

"Energy consumption is increasing steadily."
STAGE 6

Modal Verbs & Academic Hedging

Penggunaan modal verbs (can/could/should/might) untuk menyampaikan argumen hati-hati (hedging).

"Governments should implement sustainable policies."
STAGE 7

Sound-based Articles & Prepositions

Aturan bunyi vokal/konsonan artikel ("an hour" vs "a university") serta preposisi dependen.

"An orchestra played at a university."
FASE 3 • COMBOS & CLAUSE LINKING Reward: +300 XP

Verb Patterns & Penggabungan Klausa

Hubungkan beberapa verba dan klausa dalam satu kalimat tanpa menyebabkan *sentence structure crash*!

STAGE 8

Infinitive vs Gerund (Verb Patterns)

Pola 'to + infinitive' vs '-ing' setelah verb tertentu. Mencegah kesalahan *"need to managing"*.

"They need to manage money after starting work."
STAGE 9

Phrasal Verbs & Causatives

Penggunaan kata kerja phrasal & pola kausatif (make / let / help + bare verb).

"This policy helps students improve their skills."
STAGE 10

Clause Combining & Relatives

Koordinasi (FANBOYS), subordinasi (Although, Because), dan relative clauses (who/which/that) untuk Band 7+.

"Although costs rose, demand remained high."
FASE 4 • ADVANCED PRECISION Reward: +200 XP

Grammar Presisi & Kombinasi Lanjutan

Menguasai passive voice akademik, present perfect, serta teknik nominalisasi untuk meningkatkan skor Lexical & GRA.

STAGE 11

Passive Shield & Perfect Tenses

Penggunaan Passive Voice untuk nada akademik formal yang objektif dan Present Perfect.

"A new policy has been implemented by the city."
STAGE 12

Parallel Structure & Nominalization

Pengubahan kata kerja aksi menjadi noun phrase formal (e.g. manage $\rightarrow$ management).

"The management of financial resources is essential."
FASE 5 • OUTPUT AUTOMATION Reward: +300 XP

Ubah Pemahaman Pasif ke Produksi Refleks

Mengatasi sindrom "paham saat membaca, tapi kaku saat menulis/bicara" dengan drill transformasi cepat dan self-correction loops.

STAGE 13

Sentence Transformation Drills

Latihan mengubah kalimat sederhana menjadi kalimat kompleks akademik secara spontan.

Drill: Transform "People throw trash" $\rightarrow$ "Waste disposal must be regulated."
STAGE 14

Timed Production & Bug Hunting

Mendeteksi double conjunction, fragment, dan run-on sentence dalam waktu singkat.

Write $\rightarrow$ Spot Glitch $\rightarrow$ Explain Bug $\rightarrow$ Rebuild
